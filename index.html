OPEN RESEARCH INITIATIVE

NewsNLI Dataset

Currently building NewsNLI, a large-scale multilingual Natural Language Inference (NLI) dataset designed to advance reasoning capabilities across diverse languages.

Rather than simply scaling the number of examples, this initiative focuses on reasoning quality and cultural authenticity. We are collecting annotations directly from native speakers across 8 languages (Text & Audio), who not only validate semantic relationships but also provide spoken reasoning explaining their decisions.
