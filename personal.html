About Me

I'm Anuj — a pre-final year B.Tech student based in Delhi, India, studying Artificial Intelligence. When I'm not working on NLP research or building tools, I enjoy exploring the intersections of technology and human experience. I believe in learning by doing, building in public, and sharing knowledge openly.

I'm currently an Independent Researcher at ML Collective, where I've had the incredible opportunity to collaborate with researchers across continents — from Nigeria to Germany — on problems that matter for underserved language communities.
